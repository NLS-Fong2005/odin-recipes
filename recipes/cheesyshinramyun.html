Cheesy Shin Ramyun


Description

Simple shin Ramyun with melted cheese over top.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack Shin Ramyun
  • 1 Sliced Cheddar Cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook your Shin Ramyun noodle of choice following the pack instructions.
  2. Place the cheese slice on top the cooked ramyun and let it melt - we recommend using kraft cheese.
